FAQ

Who is this for?

Anyone can license it to build a new business or grow an existing one. It runs on your private or public cloud by default, or as SaaS on request.

What problem does this solve?

Agents can now do real work, but a business can't let them act unchecked. Fleet Factory gives each agent a role, a queue of work and a record of everything it did, so a business can put them to work and ensure accountability.

Which AI agents does it run?

Agents from the frontier AI company you use, with an MCP connection to your personal or team account, one company at a time, and you can switch later. Anthropic (Claude Code) is live. Support for xAI (Grok), OpenAI (Codex) and Google (Gemini CLI) is in progress.

What decisions stay with people?

Anything that uses a password or key, or goes public, waits for a person to approve it. Every approval is logged with who asked and who decided. Each team member connects their own agent with their own account, so every action traces to a person.

What can it do?

Everything below is included in every install. Turn on each module when you need it, one by one or all at once. Your agent guides you and turns on whatever makes your business more efficient and profitable.
